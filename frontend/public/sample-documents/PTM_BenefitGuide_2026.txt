PT TEKNOLOGI MAJU - EMPLOYEE GROUP HEALTH BENEFITS GUIDE 2026
Plan Name: MajuCare Plus (Group Health Insurance)
Insurer: Allianz Indonesia
Policy Number: TM-EMP-2024-001 / GHI-123456
Effective Period: 1 Jan 2024 – Present
Eligible Members: Full-time employees, spouse, children (as per plan)

SUMMARY OF BENEFITS:
1. Inpatient Care:
   - Annual Limit: IDR 500,000,000 / year
   - Room & Board: Shared room, private room eligible
   - Cashless hospital admission at network private hospitals

2. Outpatient Care:
   - Annual Limit: IDR 10,000,000 / year
   - GP and Specialist consultation with prescribed medicine
   - Cashless at network clinics or reimbursement within 30 days

3. Dental Care (Page 6 · Dental Benefits):
   - Annual Limit: IDR 2,000,000 / year
   - Covers preventive dental care including routine check-up, scaling, and basic filling, with annual limit of IDR 2,000,000.
   - Applies to: outpatient and dental clinic visits within the network

4. Optical Care:
   - Not covered under standard company health plan

5. General Exclusions (Page 14 · Exclusions):
   - Orthodontic treatment, cosmetic dental procedures, and dental implants are excluded from coverage.
   - Cosmetic surgeries, unauthorized experimental treatments, and non-prescribed therapies are not covered.

6. Maternity Care:
   - Limit: IDR 50,000,000 / event
   - Prenatal visits, normal delivery, and medically indicated C-section

6. Emergency & Accident:
   - As per plan, comprehensive 24/7 ER admission
